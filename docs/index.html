Keep what matters smooth, without closing anything.

Coremium lives in your Mac's notch. While you play, render, code or run a local model, it moves everything else to the efficiency cores. You choose which apps step aside.

Download for Mac Read the source

Free and open source. No account, no network access. macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

Performance coresEfficiency cores

Without Coremium, everything competes for the fast cores.

Four choices for every app.

Coremium picks sensible defaults from what each app is. Change any of them. Your choice always beats the mode.

Boost
Protected. When you use it, everything else is kept out of its way. Your game, your editor, your render.
Normal
Left alone. Coremium never touches it.
Yield
Steps aside to the efficiency cores while something else is boosted. Browsers, chat, sync apps.
Eco
Always efficient, unless you're using it. Backups, and anything you never need fast.

It tells you why.

Automatic mode watches what you're doing and says what it changed. Pause puts everything back to macOS defaults at once. Advanced shows the numbers.

What it doesn't do.

It never closes anything.No quitting, no freezing, no root helper. It only touches your own user's apps, and has no network access.

It can't push an app higher.macOS has no public way to raise an app's priority, so Boost works by clearing the way for it. On a Mac that isn't overloaded there is little to move.

It makes no promises it can't measure.No frame rates, battery life or temperature claims. It shows how long a boost ran and how many processes it moved.

Install in a minute.

Download the disk image

  1. Open the disk image and drag Coremium onto Applications.
  2. Open it. Coremium is signed without a paid Apple developer account, so macOS asks once: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.
  3. Follow the short tour, then hover the notch.

Checksums are published with every release.

Prefer Terminal? This downloads the latest release, checks its checksum and installs it, with no warning from macOS.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Cubinghackerz/Coremium/master/scripts/install.sh | bash

If something stays slow, this puts every app back:

/Applications/Coremium.app/Contents/MacOS/Coremium --restore-all
